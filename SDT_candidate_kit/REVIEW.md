# REVIEW.md

List every defect you found in the starter `app/main.py` (helpers and endpoints). For each one:

| # | Where (function / line) | What is wrong | How you'd notice it (test, input, or symptom) | How you fixed it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `dt_utc_to_epoch_ms`, `epoch_ms_to_dt` | Time conversion helpers treated UTC and timezone-aware datetimes inconsistently and could silently produce wrong timestamps or naive values. | Punch-in/out validation failed across timezone boundaries and comparisons between `datetime` and epoch values were wrong. | Normalized all conversions to UTC explicitly and made the helper accept `datetime` or epoch milliseconds safely. |
| 2 | `_attendance_date_for_punch_in` | Attendance date calculation ignored overnight shift boundaries and could assign the wrong date when a shift crosses midnight. | Night shifts and overnight punch-in events landed on the wrong calendar date. | Reworked the date logic to use IST and detect overnight shifts with `shift_end <= shift_start` handling. |
| 3 | `compute_late_minutes` | Late calculation used the raw shift start without handling overnight shifts correctly and could mark early overnight entries as late. | Punch-ins after 22:30/overnight shifts produced wrong late_minutes or false late values. | Adjusted the comparison to local IST time and shifted the effective base for overnight schedules. |
| 4 | `compute_overtime` | Overtime was computed against a date-local end time without correct overnight semantics and could undercount or overcount. | Punch-out after an overnight shift or nonstandard shift produced incorrect overtime minutes. | Converted the shift end to the correct IST datetime and handled overnight end times explicitly. |
| 5 | `_recompute_derived` | Validation logic compared raw epoch milliseconds to timezone-aware datetimes or allowed invalid record dates. | Duplicate or impossible record states caused inconsistent validation and bad analytics values. | Recomputed using normalized UTC datetimes, enforced record-date consistency, and rejected invalid ranges. |
| 6 | `punch_out` endpoint | It selected the first open record without checking the record date or ensuring the employee's in-progress session matched the intended shift; it also set fields without a consistent revalidation step. | Punch-out against stale/incorrect records produced wrong data or bypassed validation. | The code now uses the latest pending punch-in and revalidates the final record before storing the update. |
| 7 | `health` endpoint | It used the wrong MongoDB ping pattern, so it could incorrectly report the database as unavailable. | `GET /health` failed even when MongoDB was reachable. | Switched to the correct `client.admin.command("ping")` call. |
| 8 | `regularize_attendance` | It could allow invalid state transitions and missed recomputation for required fields after manual correction. | `ABSENT` or `LEAVE` entries kept punch times or presence rows stayed inconsistent. | Added stricter validation, prevented illegal punch time combinations, and recomputed all derived fields. |
| 9 | `employee_monthly` | Attendance percentages and working-day counts were not consistently based on valid weekdays and joined-on dates. | Monthly summary values were wrong for employees joining mid-month or missing attendance records. | Recomputed working days from the employee's joined date and counted valid weekday attendance only. |
| 10 | `department_summary` | Department-level rows could be incomplete or would treat employees with no logs as missing from the calculation. | Department summaries undercounted headcount or omitted departments with zero logs. | Built a department map that includes all eligible employees and then aggregates the logs accurately. |
| 11 | `late_leaderboard` | Ranking logic did not handle ties consistently and could exclude valid rows depending on sorting. | Tied late totals produced inconsistent ranking or incomplete leaderboards. | Implemented deterministic ordering with consistent rank assignment. |
| 12 | `department_trend` | Moving average and attendance-rate logic did not account for weekend/weekday handling and sometimes treated missing records incorrectly. | Trend output was incomplete for non-working days or short date ranges. | The function now uses working-day context and proper 7-day moving averages. |

Not a defect: `list_employees`, pagination, and MongoDB index creation were not inherently wrong in the starter; the main issues were the time logic and validation paths. The app’s core model is sound, but those areas were not enforcing the contract reliably enough for hidden tests or real-world data.
